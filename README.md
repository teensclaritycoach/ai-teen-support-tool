# AI Teen Support Tool 🧠

## Overview

The AI Teen Support Tool is a beginner-level AI project exploring how artificial intelligence can be used to provide structured educational and personal-development guidance for teenagers.

The project is inspired by my experience working with teenagers through youth development and coaching activities.

## Problem

Teenagers face different challenges as they navigate school, friendships, identity, social media, decision-making, and personal growth.

Access to helpful guidance is not always immediate or easily available.

This project explores how AI can provide a simple first point of guidance while encouraging teenagers to seek appropriate support from trusted adults, teachers, counsellors, parents, or other qualified professionals when necessary.

## Project Goal

The goal is to develop a simple AI-assisted tool that can:

* Identify the general area of concern a teenager is experiencing
* Provide age-appropriate educational guidance
* Suggest practical reflection or learning activities
* Encourage healthy decision-making
* Direct users toward trusted human support when appropriate

## Example Areas

The tool may cover areas such as:

* 📚 Academic challenges
* 👥 Friendships and peer pressure
* 📱 Social media
* 🎯 Goals and personal development
* 🧭 Identity and decision-making
* 💬 Communication
* ⏰ Time management

## AI Component

The project will explore the use of AI to understand a user's input and generate structured, educational responses based on predefined guidance categories.

The project is being developed as a learning exercise while I build my skills in artificial intelligence and technology.

## Responsible AI

This tool is **not intended to replace parents, teachers, counsellors, mental-health professionals, or other qualified support systems**.

It should avoid collecting unnecessary personal information and should encourage users to seek trusted human support when a situation requires it.

## Future Development

Future versions may include:

* A simple web interface
* AI-powered response generation
* User input categorisation
* Educational resource recommendations
* A feedback mechanism
* Safety and responsible-AI safeguards
